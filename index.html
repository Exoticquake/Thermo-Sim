🔭 Heat-Collector Ray-Trace Simulator

Real Monte-Carlo ray tracing (BVH-accelerated, Three.js). All dimensions from the Thermodynamics B&C rules. Score = 100 × (weighted energy reaching the water) / (total emitted energy).

Lamp / Heat Source

Rules: lamp height 400-500mm (regionals). Beam angle is the biggest real-world unknown — 90° = bare bulb, ~60-75° = typical clamp-lamp reflector.

Cube / Cup / Sleeve (from rules)

Cup bottom height = where the cup sits above the testing surface (raise it to simulate a riser/stand — note this eats into the reflector's own usable height, since the cube ceiling doesn't move).
Cup-wall credit = fraction of energy landing on the sleeve/cup exterior that's assumed to still reach the water (conduction). 1.0 = as good as a direct hit, 0 = totally wasted.

Upload one or more STL pieces (mm units, Y = up, cup sits on the shared vertical axis). Every uploaded piece is transformed by its own X/Y/Z position + rotation below and simulated together as one assembly — exactly what you need for a multi-part design like quarter-panels.
⚠️ Most CAD tools (OpenSCAD, Fusion360, SolidWorks...) export Z-up, not Y-up. If your shape looks sideways/wrong in the 3D view, set Rotation X = -90 on each part first — that's the standard fix.
